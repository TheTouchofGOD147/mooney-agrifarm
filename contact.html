Before contacting us

Common questions about inquiries and supply

01

Can I place an order directly through this form?

The form begins an inquiry. Final availability, quantity, pricing, timing, and fulfillment conditions must be confirmed separately.

02

Do you accept wholesale inquiries?

Yes. Include the product, estimated quantity, frequency, location, preferred date, and important quality requirements.

03

Can I discuss a supply or distribution partnership?

Yes. Provide information about your company, capabilities, location, proposed relationship, and the value you believe the partnership may create.

04

How quickly will I receive a response?

General inquiries may receive a faster response, while product, wholesale, livestock, or partnership requests may require additional review.
